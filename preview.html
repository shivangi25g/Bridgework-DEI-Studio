Behind the Door, Behind the Dashboard.

A personal, interactive website arguing that DEI is not only awareness. It is the practice of redesigning care, work, learning, and systems so people are not left to overcome barriers alone.

What I built

A website that makes DEI something people can enter, play with, listen to, and test.

Core experience

The project moves from games into two connected lenses: Shivangi's frontline care lens and Vivek's strategy, data, and systems lens. It then brings in voices, theory, allyship, and a final argument.

Opening interaction

The site begins with games so the topic feels approachable and connected to practice.

DEI Word Search

Visitors find words like equity, ally, access, care, trust, and bias to build shared language.

Care Lens Bingo

A barrier-focused game asks visitors to notice how documents, trauma, transport, privacy, and trust can cluster.

Care + Strategy Scenarios

Scenario cards ask visitors to choose responses that reduce barriers instead of blaming individuals.

Say this: I wanted the first action to be participation, not reading a wall of text. The games lower defensiveness while still teaching DEI language and behaviour.

Two lenses

Behind the Door and Behind the Dashboard turn experience into DEI simulations.

The care lens moves through a shelter-inspired path: intake, dorm, dining, mDEIcal, quiet, supply, staff, and leadership spaces. The strategy lens asks who is counted, who benefits, and who may be left out when systems, dashboards, services, and operations are designed.
